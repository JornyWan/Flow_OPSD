# Phase8 visual review

Use the same strict definitions as phase7/REVIEW_RUBRIC.md. Correct text is the
specified word case-insensitively with no missing/extra characters or other
visible text; plate lettering counts. Stylized but identifiable letters pass,
unclear characters are unknown. Record every encoded clause, full object
visibility, literal visible text and concrete notes. Relation is subject relative
to object; an object mainly below another is not clearly right merely because
its center has a small positive horizontal offset.

Exact phase7 pixel matches inherit the frozen phase7 observation and provenance.
Review every other unique image without mode, proxy score or prior outcome.
Unknown never counts as repair. Freeze observations before joining the key.
Repair retention requires previously repaired and initially correct clauses,
plus full visibility when the old target was fully visible. Continue reporting
remaining old visibility defects separately for the other three cases.

Do not change old labels after seeing new modes. Flag any inconsistency in the
report. This is a provisional arm-blinded implementing-assistant review; old
image familiarity prevents interpreting it as independent human annotation.
