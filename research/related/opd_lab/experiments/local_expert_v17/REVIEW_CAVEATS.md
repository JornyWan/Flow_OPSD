objects, cropped legs/handles/rims or a sign covering the upper bowl rim fail.

are complete. Extra spoons do not change the encoded exact apple/bowl counts;
flat panel shapes without identifiable cup openings are not invented cup objects.
Ridged cup bodies are not assumed to be stacks without evidence of distinct cups.

All observations must freeze before key join. Unknown comparisons cannot supply
safe targets under the predeclared rule. Visibility-only gains are not repairs of
an encoded clause. Labels will not be revised after identities or scores are known.
