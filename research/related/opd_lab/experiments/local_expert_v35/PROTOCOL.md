No image reads by the coding agent, no image/model inference, no GPU use, no new diffusion training. Reuse Phase34 frozen automatic evidence and350 provisional assistant observations (70 pairs /53 prompts). No new annotations and no modification to Phase34 or earlier archives.

This is a retrospective policy study. All reference images and aggregate findings were already observed in Phase34; known failure examples motivated the hypothesis. A split made now cannot create a pristine held-out test. Call the split "internal audit", never fresh independent validation. Split the53 prompt IDs by family with hash ordering seed35035; floor(2N/3) calibration and remaining audit. Keep every available seed, all five endpoints, both query indices and both amplitudes of a prompt in one split. Do not balance positives, relocate named failures, search split seeds or tune after either split's results.

Freeze one proposed primary policy and two references before running: original = old componentwise safe-repair acceptance; text_only = original plus confirmed text false-to-true; guarded_text (primary) = text_only plus every protected object/visibility field true at BOTH endpoints. The primary deliberately rejects partial text repairs on already-unsatisfied protected fields. It does not claim to fix OCR/detector labels or certify visual safety. No class, prompt, index or amplitude exceptions; no threshold search. Preserve original upstream labels, thresholds and raw evidence.

Report acceptance, reference-confirmed safety, known harm, unknowns (may overlap harm), no-repair, precision, recall against ALL reference-safe comparisons and accepted prompt/pair counts.
