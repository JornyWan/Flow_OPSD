# Exploratory same-state action geometry

Written after Phase49 terminal scores were available; not a preregistered efficacy
test. Use all32 cases, no outcome-based subsets. From the common intervention
state compute teacher-minus-before and learned-minus-before velocity vectors.
Report cosine, target projection coefficient, residual orthogonal energy and
teacher-target MSE for both learned students. No new generation, optimization,
rewards or images inspected. Direction alignment does not prove terminal value;
the teacher itself is not uniformly useful. No gate or hyperparameter selection.
