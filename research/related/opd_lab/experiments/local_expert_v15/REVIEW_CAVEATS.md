# Notes recorded before model-key join

All observations are provisional implementing-assistant labels. This is not
independent annotation. Native views were additionally inspected for ambiguous
letters and small printed patches; uncertainty remains null rather than being
filled from the expected word. Decorative strokes versus letter-like marks can
also be unresolved.

Six exact archived pixels retain their old semantic labels. In particular, public
case36/B has an apple partly inside a bowl, although its inherited visibility label
is true. Its lower-part occlusion makes that historical judgement look permissive
under the written rubric. The old label is preserved as required, not independently
revalidated as correct. Other new images with clearly obscured object parts may
receive false or null visibility. The case36/B text and relation labels are both
false regardless, so it is not a complete scene under the inherited labels either.
This is another reason not to call the six training examples a perfect oracle.

No model identity or per-model score has been read during this review. All public
case sheets were inspected; the final draft is frozen before opening the key.
