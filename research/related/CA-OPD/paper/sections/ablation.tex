\section{Ablation: mapping the OPD-improvement design space}
\label{sec:ablation}

Beyond CA-OPD (min-max reweighting), we tested three other principled
modifications to multi-teacher OPD. All four share the reproduction setup of
\S\ref{sec:exp-setup} and differ from uniform \texttt{mopd} only in the studied
component. Table~\ref{tab:ablation} reports all arms at convergence (step 721);
the outcome maps which OPD modifications help and which hurt, and \emph{why}.

\begin{description}
  \item[\textnormal{\textbf{(1) Min-max reweighting (CA-OPD).}}] The method of
  \S\ref{sec:method}. \emph{Helps early} (Table~\ref{tab:caopd}: faster on the
  lagging aesthetics axis) but at convergence is within noise of \texttt{mopd} on
  every axis and higher on none (Aes $6.157$ vs.\ $6.172$, OCR $0.928$ vs.\
  $0.939$). Mechanism: min-max reallocates capacity toward the worst-served reward
  axis; on a near-saturated benchmark this trades a small final quality margin for
  convergence speed.

  \item[\textnormal{\textbf{(2) Huber / trust-region residual (\texttt{robust}).}}]
  Replace the squared velocity residual by a Huber loss~\cite{huber1964robust}, to
  be robust to teachers that reward-hack. \emph{Neutral}: within noise of
  \texttt{mopd} across axes, and notably the \emph{best} OCR at convergence
  ($0.951$). Mechanism: with well-behaved teachers there are few large residual
  outliers for the Huber threshold to clip, so it neither helps nor hurts
  overall---a useful confirmation that OPD's plain $L_2$ is already well-behaved
  here.

  \item[\textnormal{\textbf{(3) Min-SNR per-step weighting (\texttt{snr}).}}]
  Apply Min-SNR-$\gamma$ timestep weighting~\cite{hang2023efficient} to the
  per-step OPD loss. \emph{Hurts badly} (GenEval collapses to $0.785$, Aesthetics
  to $5.851$). Mechanism: Min-SNR down-weights high-noise steps, but in OPD those
  early, high-noise steps set the sample's global structure and compositional
  layout; suppressing them destroys GenEval and structure. This shows OPD's
  uniform per-step weighting is not an oversight but a good default.

  \item[\textnormal{\textbf{(4) SDE$\to$ODE noise annealing (\texttt{anneal}).}}]
  Start distillation in the stochastic SDE regime and anneal to the deterministic
  ODE. \emph{Hurts most} (HPS $0.298$ and Aesthetics $5.623$ degrade sharply).
  Mechanism: the early high-noise SDE phase injects variance that degrades the
  student before annealing can help, directly confirming the base paper's finding
  that the ODE objective dominates SDE.
\end{description}

\paragraph{Takeaway.}
The design space splits cleanly: reweighting \emph{across teachers} (min-max,
Huber) stays within noise of the strong uniform baseline, whereas reweighting
\emph{across denoising steps} (Min-SNR) or perturbing the \emph{sampler regime}
(SDE annealing) is actively harmful. This localizes where OPD improvements can
plausibly come from---teacher combination, not per-step or sampler
manipulation---and shows CA-OPD's early-convergence benefit is not a trivial
artifact of any reweighting.
