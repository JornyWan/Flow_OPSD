\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

We first recap the base DiffusionOPD objective (\S\ref{sec:m-recap}), then
present CA-OPD, our conflict-averse multi-teacher reweighting
(\S\ref{sec:m-caopd}). The full multi-objective theory---including the
Pareto/reward-space analysis, the envelope-theorem exactness result, and the
commensurability property---is developed in \S\ref{sec:theory}, which this
section inputs directly.

\subsection{Recap: DiffusionOPD}
\label{sec:m-recap}

We adopt DiffusionOPD~\cite{li2026diffusionopd} verbatim as our base method. A
flow-matching student $v_\theta$ rolls out its own reverse-time trajectory
$x_{t_0},\dots,x_{t_N}$ ($1{=}t_0{>}\cdots{>}t_N{=}0$) via the Euler--Maruyama
discretization of Flow-GRPO~\cite{liu2025flow}. At each step, the student and a
frozen teacher $v_\phi$ define one-step Gaussian transition kernels that (i) are
evaluated at the \emph{same} on-policy state $x_{t_j}$ and (ii) share the
\emph{same} covariance $\bar\sigma_j^2 I_d$ (a function only of the scheduler and
global noise level). They therefore differ only in mean, and the per-step reverse
KL is closed-form,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:m-kl}
\mathrm{KL}\!\bigl(\mathcal N(\mu_S,\bar\sigma_j^2 I)\,\Vert\,\mathcal N(\mu_T,\bar\sigma_j^2 I)\bigr)
=\frac{\|\mu_S(x_{t_j})-\mu_T(x_{t_j})\|_2^2}{2\bar\sigma_j^2}
\;\propto\;\|v_\theta-v_\phi\|_2^2 ,
\end{equation}
introducing no Monte-Carlo variance. This yields dense, pathwise per-step
velocity matching; in the deterministic ODE regime (the default) it becomes the
direct $L_2$ loss $\tfrac12\|\mu_S-\mu_T\|_2^2$~\cite{li2026diffusionopd}.

\paragraph{Uniform multi-teacher OPD (mopd).}
Given $M$ frozen task teachers $\{v_{\phi_m}\}_{m=1}^M$, DiffusionOPD defines the
per-teacher loss $\mathcal L_m(\theta)$ from \eqref{eq:m-kl} and trains on their
\emph{uniform} average, implemented as round-robin gradient accumulation with
factor $G=M$:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:m-mopd}
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{mopd}}(\theta)=\frac1M\sum_{m=1}^M \mathcal L_m(\theta).
\end{equation}
As we show in \S\ref{sec:theory}, the minimizer of \eqref{eq:m-mopd} drives the
student velocity toward the per-step teacher mean $\tfrac1M\sum_m v_{\phi_m}$.

\subsection{CA-OPD: conflict-averse multi-teacher reweighting}
\label{sec:m-caopd}

CA-OPD replaces the uniform average \eqref{eq:m-mopd} by an
entropy-regularized Tchebycheff (min-max) scalarization over per-teacher
reference-normalized losses. Because per-batch losses are noisy, we drive the
weights from an exponential moving average of each teacher's per-step KL,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:m-ema}
\bar\ell_m^{(t)}=\beta\,\bar\ell_m^{(t-1)}+(1-\beta)\,\mathcal L_m(\theta^{(t)}),
\qquad
w_m^{(t)}=\frac{\exp\!\bigl(\bar\ell_m^{(t)}/(\tau r_m)\bigr)}{\sum_{k}\exp\!\bigl(\bar\ell_k^{(t)}/(\tau r_k)\bigr)},
\end{equation}
and take the \emph{detached}-weight loss
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:m-caopd}
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{CA\text{-}OPD}}^{(t)}(\theta)=\sum_{m=1}^M \mathrm{sg}\!\bigl[w_m^{(t)}\bigr]\,\mathcal L_m(\theta),
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{sg}[\cdot]$ is stop-gradient. This up-weights the currently
\emph{worst-matched} (largest EMA-KL) teacher. Two properties, proved in
\S\ref{sec:theory}, make this principled rather than heuristic: (i) by the
envelope (Danskin) theorem, \eqref{eq:m-caopd} is the \emph{exact} gradient of
the smooth log-sum-exp Tchebycheff surrogate
$\mathcal F_\tau(\theta)=\tau\log\sum_m\exp(\mathcal L_m/(\tau r_m))$ (no term
through the softmax is missed); and (ii) as $\tau\to\infty$, $w_m\to1/M$ and CA-OPD
reduces \emph{exactly} to mopd \eqref{eq:m-mopd}, so uniform DiffusionOPD is the
$\tau\to\infty$ special case and lies inside CA-OPD's search space. The
temperature $\tau$ controls how aggressively the worst teacher is prioritized.

\paragraph{Implementation.}
CA-OPD is a small addition to the DiffusionOPD trainer and changes nothing about
teacher training or the rollout. In each round-robin cycle we tag each
accumulated micro-batch with its \texttt{teacher\_idx}, maintain the per-teacher
EMA \texttt{teacher\_kl\_ema}, and scale each teacher's contribution by the
detached softmax weight \eqref{eq:m-ema} before the single backward pass. We use
$\tau{=}0.5$ and $\beta{=}0.9$; the reference $r_m$ is fixed. Setting the flag off
recovers the unmodified mopd baseline exactly (\texttt{config.train.ca\_opd}, with
\texttt{ca\_opd\_tau} and \texttt{ca\_opd\_ema} defaulting via \texttt{getattr}),
so the two arms differ only in the weighting of an otherwise identical loss.
Algorithm~\ref{alg:caopd} summarizes Stage~2; Stage~1 (per-task teacher training)
is unchanged from DiffusionOPD.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{CA-OPD (Stage~2 multi-teacher on-policy distillation)}
\label{alg:caopd}
\small
\KwIn{Teachers $\{v_{\phi_m}\}_{m=1}^M$; student $v_\theta\!\leftarrow\!v^{\mathrm{ref}}$; temperature $\tau$; EMA $\beta$; refs $\{r_m\}$.}
Initialize EMA $\bar\ell_m\!\leftarrow\!0$\;
\For{each training round}{
  \For{$m=1,\dots,M$}{
    Sample prompts $\vc\sim\mathcal C^{(m)}$; roll out student to get $\{x_{t_j}\}$ \tcp*{\texttt{no\_grad}}
    Compute $\mathcal L_m(\theta)$ from Eq.~\eqref{eq:m-kl} (ODE $L_2$ by default)\;
    Update EMA: $\bar\ell_m\!\leftarrow\!\beta\bar\ell_m+(1-\beta)\,\mathrm{detach}(\mathcal L_m)$\;
  }
  Weights $w_m\!\leftarrow\!\mathrm{softmax}_m(\bar\ell_m/(\tau r_m))$ \tcp*{detached}
  Backprop $\sum_m \mathrm{sg}[w_m]\,\mathcal L_m$ and take one optimizer step\;
}
\end{algorithm}
